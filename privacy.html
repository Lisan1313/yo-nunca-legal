Never Ever privacy policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026 · Leer en español

Summary: Never Ever does not collect any of your data. Everything you type into the app stays stored on your phone. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no ads, no servers of ours, and we do not send your information anywhere.

What is stored and where

The app stores the following, only in your phone's storage:

  • The players' names, if you choose to type them. They are optional and are used to put them into some questions and into the end-of-game summary.
  • The questions you add yourself under “My questions”.
  • Which cards have already come up, so they are not repeated.
  • Your settings: language, sound, vibration, dares and bomb mode.

That information never leaves the device. We have no access to it, and neither does anyone else. The app works without an internet connection.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not ask for your name, your email or any personal data.
  • There are no user accounts and no sign-up.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • We do not share, sell or hand over data to third parties, because we have none.
  • We do not store or send what each person answers during the game.

Other people's names

If you type your friends' names to play, those names stay only on your phone, like everything else. You can remove them at any time from the home screen.

Sharing

The app lets you share a link to its store page and, once available, the game summary. Only what you choose is shared, and only when you tap, through the app you pick (messaging, email, and so on). From that point on, that other app's terms apply.

Permissions

The app does not ask for sensitive permissions: it does not use the camera, the microphone, your location, your contacts or your photos. It only uses the phone's vibration and sound for game effects, and you can turn them off in Settings.

In-app purchases

The app is free and has one optional paid item bought once (Spicy mode). If you buy it, the payment is handled by Google Play. We never see or store your card details. Google only tells us whether the purchase is active for your account. There are no subscriptions.

Age

The app is not directed at children under 13. Spicy mode is for adults only: the app asks you to confirm that every player is 18 or older before turning it on. We do not collect data from anyone, regardless of age, and we do not store that confirmation.

How to delete your data

There are two ways, both immediate and permanent:

  • Inside the app: Settings → Delete my data.
  • Uninstalling the app: everything it stored on the phone is deleted.

There is no need to ask us for anything, because we hold no copy.

Changes to this policy

If the app changed and started handling data differently, we would update this page and say so in the app itself before it happens.
